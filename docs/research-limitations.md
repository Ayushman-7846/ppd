# Research limitations

- **Sampling.** The teaching sets and the candidate pilot are authored. Their scores illustrate the application. Independent annotation, broader difficulty coverage and fresh items are needed for claims about deployed systems.
- **Contamination.** Published stimuli can reappear in training data. Preserve the source and publication date and reserve unpublished held-out cases where research practice permits.
- **Prompts and provider drift.** Both prompts and effective settings affect the output. Providers can omit the serving version or change an alias. Availability, refusals, and rate limits affect coverage.
- **Variability.** Repeats can disagree under the same recorded settings. Stable exact text or a stable parse can agree on an incorrect answer. Embedding cosine describes vector similarity under one configured model.
- **Grading.** Formal validators cover bounded structure and supported rule application. Equivalent analyses, grammatical expansions and explanations require judgment. AI-assisted judging can be inconsistent. Human reviewers may disagree or infer the producer from the text.
- **Inference.** Percentile intervals assume independent stimuli. Missing decisive or weighted observations limit inference. Report the cohort and its sample sizes. Effects estimated across the sampled prompts and models are observational.
- **Interpretation.** Elicited language analysis is behavioral evidence. Human-like understanding would require additional experimental evidence.
- **Engineering.** Cohort computation is limited to 2,000 responses. Live credentials, model access, Ollama and deployment environments have separate readiness gates. [Final testing report](final-testing-report.md)
